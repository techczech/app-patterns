Four captured views of the existing settings component demo, composed as a screenshot collage. The screenshots are unmodified full browser captures; the collage crops each to the settings window.

Frames were selected through the demo’s `window.__go` selector: Settings frame 1 (index 0), Search frame 2 (index 1), Keyboard shortcuts frame 7 (index 6), Change history frame 6 (index 5).
